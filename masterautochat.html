FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about Master Autochat.

What is Master Autochat?
Master Autochat is a social media chat automation platform that lets businesses automate conversations on Instagram, WhatsApp, Facebook, TikTok, and SMS to capture leads, answer queries, and drive sales — 24/7.
Do I need coding skills?
Not at all! Our drag-and-drop visual flow builder is designed for anyone. If you can use a smartphone, you can build automations on Master Autochat.
Which social platforms are supported?
We support Instagram, WhatsApp Business, Facebook Messenger, TikTok, SMS, and Email. More channels are being added regularly.
Is it approved by Meta and TikTok?
Yes! Master Autochat uses only official platform APIs and complies fully with Meta Business Policies and TikTok's messaging guidelines.
Can I try it for free?
Absolutely. Our Free plan includes up to 500 contacts and 10 automation flows — forever free, no credit card required.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes, you can cancel your paid plan at any time. Your data is retained for 30 days after cancellation so you can export everything.
